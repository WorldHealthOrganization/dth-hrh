Glossary of the Digital Transformation Handbook for Human Resources for Health and Health Worker Registries
Definition
The handbook's health workforce terms (digital terms are addressed separately). One concept per term, each related to the candidate definitions in twg-candidates.glossary.json. A term becomes 'authored' only when the working group adopts a definition: a person sets `definition` and `status`, citing the change set that decided it in `scopeNote`. Re-running scripts/glossary/build_glossary.py keeps authored terms. Organizations several handbooks name live once in smart-ra's organizations scheme (litlfred/smart-ra#198) and are listed under members; organization terms here carry a foaf:focus to a W3C ORG organization (litlfred/folio-assistant-core#411).
