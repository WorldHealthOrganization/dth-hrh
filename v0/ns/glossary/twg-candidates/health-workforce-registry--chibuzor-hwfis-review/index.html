Health workforce registry (en)
Definition
"HWR or HRH registry is a 'single authoritative source of health workforce information, that can provide an accurate count of all health care personnel that either has worked or are currently working at national or sub-national levels, including in the private sector'" (World Health Organization. Monitoring the building blocks of health systems: a handbook of indicators and their measurement strategies.) "The HWR does not function in isolation. It is a part of the Human Resource Information System (HRIS) (also called health workforce information system). HRIS is a system that collects, maintains and analyses data on the health workforce. Other components of HRIS are payroll, training, performance management,migration, benefits and retirement components (Fig. 1). The HWR is, however, a central component of the HRIS and provides data on which all the other components depend. (en)
