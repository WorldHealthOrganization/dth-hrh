Health workforce registry (en)
Definition
Glossary: "Health Worker Registry: a national authority of health worker information according to a defined minimum data set." "Provider Registry: Another synonym for Health Worker Registry." "Health Worker Directory: List of health workers that are managed by individual organizations, which can come together to form a health worker registry." Narrative definition: "A Health Worker Registry (HWR) serves as the digital, central authority for maintaining the unique virtual identities of health workers within a country. . The Health Worker Registry pulls data from underlying human resource information systems (HRIS), which play the role of Health Worker Directories, and makes these data available as a single virtual database. These Health Worker Directories could include Ministry of Health installations of tools like iHRIS, private systems used by faith-based organizations, and private-sector systems as well as those used by regulatory bodies like nursing councils. These health Worker directories are responsible for specific business processes: maybe one tracks health worker certifications for a professional council or another holds salary information for the ministry of health. The information stored in these directories mirrors their business cases. The Health Worker Registry has no business case other than being a master document of all health worker information that specific directories can pull and share. The Health Worker Registry contains a Minimum Data Set (MDS) of details of all health workers working in both public and private sectors, such as full name, contact information and place(s) of employment. The Health Worker Registry (HWR) serves as the authority for maintaining the unique identities of health workers within a country (p12) Governments collect and store multiple and disparate sources of data on health workers. Thus, it is a complex task to pull together and maintain a master list of all health workers in a country. A Health Worker Registry (HWR) seeks to reduce the complexity of this task by: ● creating a Minimum Data Set (MDS) of health workforce information from the various source data systems ● merging the information from a variety of data systems into an authoritative registry of health workers according to a data governance policy ● allowing queries of health worker information by various computer and human users, also called the client systems. The HWR serves as a publicly available “electronic phone-book” of health workers and health services across all sectors (public, private and faith-based organizations). As the World Health Organization points out, the HWR can be seen as the “single authoritative source” of health workforce information that parts of the HIS can query to obtain details on health workers and automatically populate their databases, rather than having to manually enter those details themselves. (en)
