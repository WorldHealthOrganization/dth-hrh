Also called

  • NUID (en)

Definition
An autonomous statutory body formed by the Government of India under the Indian Nursing Council Act, 1947 (Act No. 48 of 1947), operating under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare alongside the National Medical Commission. (en)
Code
SEAR / India
Scope note
Regulates: Nursing. Mandate: Registers nurses at the national level (including via the Nurses Registration & Tracking System, a live national registry); standardizes nursing curricula; recognizes and inspects nursing education institutions; sets professional-compliance standards.
