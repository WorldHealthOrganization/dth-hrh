Superintendence of Health (Superintendencia de Salud) (en)
Definition
A decentralized government regulatory body operating under the Ministry of Health, governed by DFL No. 1 of 2005; functions as a unified, cross-profession registrar rather than a profession-specific council (Chile's medical and nursing colegios are voluntary professional associations, not statutory licensing bodies). (en)
Code
AMR / Chile
Scope note
Regulates: All regulated health professions (unified national registry, not profession-specific). Mandate: Manages the National Registry of Individual Health Providers (Registro Nacional de Prestadores Individuales de Salud) covering all regulated health professions; verifies professional qualifications; oversees public safety standards. Reference: Decreto Fuerza de Ley (DFL) N° 1 de 2005 Checked 2026-10-14: not verified (source pages could not be opened; web search only).
