Jordan Medical Council (JMC) (en)
Also called
JMC (en)
Definition
A body with its own legal personality and financial/administrative independence, headed by the Minister of Health, established under Jordanian medical council legislation approved by parliament in 2022. Note: the precise official statute number/gazette citation should be verified directly with JMC or the Jordanian Official Gazette before use in the handbook. (Nursing is regulated separately — see the Jordanian Nursing Council row added below.) (en)
Code
EMR / Jordan
Scope note
Regulates: Medicine. Mandate: Verifies medical credentials of Jordanian and foreign-trained doctors; administers (or grants defined exemptions from) the Jordanian licensing examination; grants physicians the legal privilege to practise medicine in Jordan; sets category-based registration requirements. Reference: 2022 Jordan Medical Council law, as reported by Jordan Times; https://jordantimes.com/news/local/lower-house-approves-2022-jordanian-medical-council-draft-law; https://dataflowgroup.com/organization/jordan-medical-council/ Checked 2026-10-14: not verified (source pages could not be opened; web search only). Suspected issues, to confirm: Note the JMC itself predates 2022 (earlier Jordan Medical Council Law); the 2022 law replaced it - earlier law number not confirmed.
