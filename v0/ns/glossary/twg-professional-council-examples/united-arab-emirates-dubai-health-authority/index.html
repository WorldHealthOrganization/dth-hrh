Definition
A public authority with independent legal personality established under Law No. (6) of 2018 Concerning the Dubai Health Authority (superseding the 2007 law that first created it); one of three UAE healthcare regulators alongside the federal Ministry of Health and Prevention (MOHAP, Northern Emirates) and the Abu Dhabi Department of Health (DoH) — the UAE has no single national medical or nursing council, and DHA licenses both doctors and nurses as a single omnibus regulator for Dubai. (en)
Code
EMR / United Arab Emirates (Dubai)
Scope note
Regulates: All licensed health professions, including medicine and nursing (single omnibus regulator for Dubai). Mandate: Authorises individuals and facilities to practise health professions in Dubai and issues professional licences; sets bylaws, standards and requirements for practice; audits and inspects practitioners and facilities for compliance; investigates complaints and alleged malpractice.
