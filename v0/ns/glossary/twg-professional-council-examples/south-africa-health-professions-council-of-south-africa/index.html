Definition
A statutory health regulatory body and a juristic person established under Section 2 of the Health Professions Act (Act No. 56 of 1974). (en)
Code
AFR / South Africa
Scope note
Regulates: Medicine and 11 other health professions (nursing and pharmacy are regulated separately by their own statutory councils). Mandate: Controls education, training, and registration for health professions; ensures practitioners maintain professional ethics; enforces compulsory Continuing Professional Development (CPD).
