Also called
DHP (en)
Definition
The sole regulatory authority for healthcare practitioners in Qatar, created by Emiri Decree No. 14 of 2019 restructuring the Ministry of Public Health and absorbing the former Qatar Council for Healthcare Practitioners (established by Emiri Decree No. 7 of 2013, building on Law No. 13 of 2002); regulates both medicine and nursing as a single omnibus body. (en)
Code
EMR / Qatar
Scope note
Regulates: All licensed healthcare practitioners, including medicine and nursing (single omnibus regulator). Mandate: Registers and licenses healthcare practitioners as the sole authority for the state; operates a Fitness to Practise function for complaints and ethical compliance; sets accreditation standards for medical education and continuing professional development.
