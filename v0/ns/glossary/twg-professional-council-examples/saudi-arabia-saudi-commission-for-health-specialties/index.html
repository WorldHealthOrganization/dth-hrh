SCFHS (en)
Definition
A Saudi professional scientific commission with an independent legal personality, established by Royal Order No. M/2 of 1992 and supervised by a Board of Trustees; functions as a single omnibus regulator covering medicine, nursing, and other health professions rather than separate profession-specific councils. (en)
Code
EMR / Saudi Arabia
Scope note
Regulates: All licensed health professions, including medicine and nursing (single omnibus regulator). Mandate: Classifies and registers health practitioners by professional rank through its Professional Practice Executive Board; accredits training programmes and healthcare specialties; sets and enforces continuing professional development and re-licensing requirements.
