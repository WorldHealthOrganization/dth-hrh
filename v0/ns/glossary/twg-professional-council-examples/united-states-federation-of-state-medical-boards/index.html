Definition
A non-profit national coordinating organization for the ~70 state and territorial medical boards that hold the actual statutory licensing authority for physicians in the United States (there is no single federal medical licensing body, mirroring NCSBN's role for nursing). (en)
Code
AMR / United States
Scope note
Regulates: Medicine (coordinating body; actual licensure is issued by individual state medical boards, not FSMB itself). Mandate: Supports state medical boards with shared services, data, and technology (e.g. co-develops the USMLE Step 3 exam with the National Board of Medical Examiners, credentials verification, uniform applications); promotes regulatory best practice and information-sharing among state boards; does not itself register or license physicians.
