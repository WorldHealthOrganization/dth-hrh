Definition
A semi-governmental professional syndicate founded in 1940, with mandatory membership required for physicians to practise. Sources consulted give conflicting founding/governing law numbers, so the precise statutory citation should be confirmed against the Egyptian Official Gazette before use in the handbook. (Nursing is regulated separately by its own syndicate — see the row added below.) (en)
Code
EMR / Egypt
Scope note
Regulates: Medicine. Mandate: Assists the Ministry of Health in conferring licences to physicians; verifies academic credentials on application and issues practice certificates; administers licence renewals and approval of foreign-trained doctors; since February 2021 shares national licensing-exam administration with the newly created Egyptian Health Council for new graduates.
