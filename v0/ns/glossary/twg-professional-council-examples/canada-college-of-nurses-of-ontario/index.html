Definition
Ontario's statutory self-regulatory body for nursing, established under the Nursing Act, 1991, operating together with the umbrella Regulated Health Professions Act, 1991 (RHPA); came into effect in 1993. (en)
Code
AMR / Canada (Ontario)
Scope note
Regulates: Nursing (Registered Nurses and Registered Practical Nurses). Mandate: Registers Registered Nurses (RNs) and Registered Practical Nurses (RPNs) across defined registration classes; defines the profession's scope of practice and protects nursing titles; authorizes controlled acts; operates a Quality Assurance Program and investigates professional misconduct. Reference: Nursing Act, 1991; Regulated Health Professions Act, 1991; https://neltoolkit.rnao.ca/sites/default/files/Legislation%20and%20Regulation%20An%20Introduction%20to%20the%20Nursing%20Act,%201991_Revised%20June%202014.pdf Checked 2026-10-14: not verified (source pages could not be opened; web search only).
