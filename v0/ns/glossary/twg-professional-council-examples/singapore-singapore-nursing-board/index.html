Singapore Nursing Board (SNB) (en)
Also called
SNB (en)
Definition
A statutory board established under the Nurses and Midwives Act 1999, operating under the Ministry of Health alongside the Singapore Medical Council. (en)
Code
WPR / Singapore
Scope note
Regulates: Nursing and midwifery, including Advanced Practice Nurses. Mandate: Approves or rejects applications for registration/enrolment of nurses and registration of midwives; issues and renews practising certificates; sets and maintains training, education, professional conduct and ethical standards; accredits nursing/midwifery education programmes; certifies Advanced Practice Nurses. Reference: Nurses and Midwives Act 1999; https://www.snb.gov.sg/who-we-are/functions-of-snb/ Checked 2026-10-14: not verified (source pages could not be opened; web search only).
