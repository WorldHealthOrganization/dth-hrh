General Medical Council (GMC) (en)
Also called
GMC (en)
Definition
The statutory regulator for doctors (and, since 2024, physician associates and anaesthesia associates) in the UK, operating under the Medical Act 1983 as amended, most recently by the Anaesthesia Associates and Physician Associates Order 2024. (en)
Code
EUR / United Kingdom
Scope note
Regulates: Medicine. Mandate: Sets the professional standards doctors must meet; approves and monitors medical education and training; determines eligibility for and maintains the register of licensed doctors (registration plus a licence to practise); investigates fitness-to-practise concerns affecting patient safety. Reference: Medical Act 1983; https://www.gmc-uk.org/about/what-we-do-and-why/our-mandate; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Medical_Council Checked 2026-10-14: not verified (source pages could not be opened; web search only).
