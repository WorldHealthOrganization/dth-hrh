Nursing Board Malaysia (Lembaga Jururawat Malaysia) (en)
Definition
Malaysia's statutory nursing regulator, established under the Nurses Act 1950 and the Nurses Registration Regulations 1985, operating under the Ministry of Health alongside the separate Malaysian Medical Council. (en)
Code
WPR / Malaysia
Scope note
Regulates: Nursing (a separate Midwives Board regulates midwifery under the Midwives Act 1966). Mandate: Keeps and maintains the Register of Nurses; regulates nursing practice and training/examination procedures; issues registration credentials; enforces professional conduct standards through an established code of ethics.
