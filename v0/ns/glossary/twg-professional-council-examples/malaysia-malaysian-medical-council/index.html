Definition
A statutory council established under Part II of the Medical Act 1971 (Act 50), which consolidated and amended the law relating to registration and practice of medical practitioners in Malaysia. (Nursing is regulated separately — see the Nursing Board Malaysia row added below.) (en)
Code
WPR / Malaysia
Scope note
Regulates: Medicine. Mandate: Maintains the Register of Medical Practitioners under Part III of the Act; approves qualifications recognized for registration; oversees provisional and full registration, including compulsory post-registration public service; enforces professional conduct standards.
