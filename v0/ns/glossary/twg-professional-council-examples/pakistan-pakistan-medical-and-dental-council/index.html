Definition
The statutory regulatory authority maintaining the official register of medical and dental practitioners in Pakistan, most recently re-established under the Pakistan Medical and Dental Council Act, 2022, after a 2019-2023 period during which its functions were held by the short-lived Pakistan Medical Commission. (Nursing is regulated separately — see the Pakistan Nursing Council row added below.) (en)
Code
EMR / Pakistan
Scope note
Regulates: Medicine and dentistry. Mandate: Sets minimum qualification and education standards for medicine and dentistry jointly with the Higher Education Commission; requires registration of all practitioners and students before legal practice; administers the national licensing/registration examination and register.
