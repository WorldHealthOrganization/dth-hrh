NCK (en)
Definition
A statutory body established by the Nurses Act, Chapter 257 of the Laws of Kenya, functioning as the sole professional regulator for nursing and midwifery cadres in Kenya, distinct from the Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council. (en)
Code
AFR / Kenya
Scope note
Regulates: Nursing and midwifery. Mandate: Registers and licenses nurses and midwives trained domestically or internationally; conducts national licensing examinations; maintains official registers; approves training institutions and curricula; conducts disciplinary proceedings and advises government on nursing matters.
