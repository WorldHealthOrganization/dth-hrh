HCPC (en)
Definition
A statutory corporation and regulatory body established under the Health Professions Order 2001 to protect the public across 15 professions (allied health and other health/care professions; does not cover doctors or nurses/midwives, who are regulated separately — see the GMC and NMC-UK rows added below). (en)
Code
EUR / United Kingdom
Scope note
Regulates: 15 allied health and other health/care professions (does not cover doctors or nurses/midwives). Mandate: Enforces protected professional titles by law; approves qualifying education programs; sets and assesses the baseline Standards of Proficiency and Conduct.
