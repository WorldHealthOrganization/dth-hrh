South African Nursing Council (SANC) (en)
Also called
SANC (en)
Definition
An autonomous statutory body established under the Nursing Act, 2005 (Act No. 33 of 2005), operating separately from the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA), which does not regulate nursing. (en)
Code
AFR / South Africa
Scope note
Regulates: Nursing. Mandate: Registers nurses and maintains a public register of qualified practitioners; determines the scope of practice and conditions of nursing practice; accredits nursing education institutions and programmes; investigates complaints and imposes disciplinary sanctions, publishing sanctions within 30 days.
