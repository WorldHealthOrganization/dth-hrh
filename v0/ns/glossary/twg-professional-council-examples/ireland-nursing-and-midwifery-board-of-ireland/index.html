Definition
The statutory regulatory body for nurses and midwives in Ireland, governed by the Nurses and Midwives Act 2011. (en)
Code
EUR / Ireland
Scope note
Regulates: Nursing and midwifery. Mandate: Protects the public and the integrity of the nursing and midwifery professions; maintains the Register of Nurses and Midwives and a Candidate Register for students; sets standards for and approves education/training programmes; investigates fitness-to-practise complaints and imposes sanctions; advises the Minister for Health on nursing/midwifery matters.
