MDCN (en)
Definition
A statutory federal regulatory body established under the Medical and Dental Practitioners Act (originally Decree No. 23 of 1988, now Cap. M8, Laws of the Federation of Nigeria 2004). (Nursing/midwifery is regulated separately — see the NMCN row added below.) (en)
Code
AFR / Nigeria
Scope note
Regulates: Medicine and dentistry. Mandate: Determines the standards of knowledge and skill required for admission to the medical/dental professions; establishes and maintains the register of practitioners entitled to practise, publishing lists of qualified practitioners; sets codes of professional conduct; regulates related fields such as laboratory and alternative medicine practice. Reference: Medical and Dental Practitioners Act, Cap.
